# Supplied image review — 14 October 2026

Reviewed all 67 JPEGs in the supplied WhatsApp folder, plus its one MP4. The palace material is a photographed **Second Coronation Anniversary programme dated 29 June 2024**. File transfer dates are not event dates.

## Published result
- 22 named leadership profiles: one paramount ruler, four first-class chiefs, seventeen third-class chiefs.
- 25 searchable archive records; original page photographs accompany readable summaries.
- Four source-based journal articles, a succession timeline, expanded royal biography and six gallery pages.
- Citation subjects are separate from territorial chiefs. No unsupported second-class appointments have been assigned.
- Source numbers refer to the alphabetically sorted input files, not the booklet’s printed pagination.
- This is a content extraction and editorial inventory, not a complete verbatim OCR transcript.

## Editorial decisions and unresolved details
- No second-class or district/ward/clan roster was supplied. Those directory filters explain the missing records.
- All officeholder descriptions are explicitly historical (June 2024). No current 2026 appointment verification is implied.
- First-class names follow the captioned roster: George Edeh, Ogah Ero, Agangwu Baba Odangala, Emmanuel Okochi. Other programme pages use Ede / Oga / shortened variants.
- Third-class roster spells Peter Eyikwodani; the committee spelling varies. Arubi Inmo Felix and the Obi/Edumoga pairing are preserved as printed and should receive Secretariat confirmation.
- Ogbadigbo in the roster is presented as Ogbadibo in navigation/search. Other printed community spellings are retained.
- Jerry Ochola remains marked Acting (2024 record).
- The first reign is printed 1947–1960 while the narrative says 23 years. Only the dates are used, with a public discrepancy note.
- The royal biography contains a service-duration inconsistency; that duration is omitted.
- Francis Alochu Ikpobu is also called Frank in the citation; both forms are noted in the archive.
- Abba/Aba Adaudu spellings vary. The archive uses Abba, as in the biography body and letter.
- Some sources have glare, incomplete edges and uncaptioned photographs. Missing text is not reconstructed.
- Personal letters were reviewed but not reproduced publicly; contact details and signatures were not extracted into site content.
- Sources 33 and 34 contain headings without substantive biographies; no profiles were invented.
- Sources 65 and 67 are unrelated personal photographs and were excluded from the public site.
- MP4 duration is 0.533 seconds, 368 × 640. Browser inspection shows a recording of the history page, not a ceremony or royal address. It adds no usable video story and is excluded from the public library.
- Original source files are unchanged. Selected page photographs were copied without retouching.
- Existing browser copy deterrents remain. Public web content cannot be made impossible to copy.
